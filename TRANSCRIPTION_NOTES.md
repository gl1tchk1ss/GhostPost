### Review policy

- Clear characters are transcribed literally.
- A `?` is preserved when a sender initial, origin, or postmark is unclear instead of guessing.
- Bracketed locations such as `[UK]`, `[Canada]`, `[Puerto Rico]`, and `[Mongolia]` are stored as `geographic_label`; GhostPost does not reinterpret the source's geography labels.
- Superscript markers are preserved in `source_marker` and `raw_entry` when legible. Their meaning has not yet been modeled.
- Rows with unclear handwriting or an uncertain superscript marker are flagged `needs_review`.
- The PDF page number and reading-order entry number form the audit locator for every row.
