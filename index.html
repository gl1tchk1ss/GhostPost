About

GhostPost is an independent, unofficial transcription of correspondence metadata published in the Luigi Mangione legal defense team's public mail catalog. It records dates, sender initials, limited location metadata, postmarks, and source-page provenance. It does not publish full sender names, addresses, or letter contents.
